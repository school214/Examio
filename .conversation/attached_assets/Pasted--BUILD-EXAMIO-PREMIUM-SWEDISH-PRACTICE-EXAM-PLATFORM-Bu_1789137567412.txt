# BUILD EXAMIO — PREMIUM SWEDISH PRACTICE-EXAM PLATFORM

Build a polished, modern Swedish web application called **Examio**.

Examio is a study and practice platform for Swedish students. It is designed to **simulate real school exams and help students practice**, but it is NOT an official exam system and must never claim that an Examio result is an official school grade.

The first target is **Årskurs 8**.

The website should feel inspired by the simplicity and seriousness of platforms such as Exam.net, but it must have its own branding, UI, functionality and design.

Do NOT build a giant complicated application immediately. Prioritize the core experience first and keep the architecture clean so more subjects and exams can be added later.

# 11. EXTREMELY GOOD AI FEEDBACK

This is one of Examio's most important features.

Do NOT only check:

"Correct / Incorrect."

For written answers, analyze the student's actual response.

The AI should consider:

### Knowledge

Does the student know the relevant facts?

### Concepts

Does the student correctly use subject-specific terminology?

### Accuracy

Are claims factually correct?

### Reasoning

Does the student explain WHY?

### Connections

Does the student connect concepts?

### Cause and consequence

Can the student explain causes and consequences?

### Comparison

Can the student identify similarities and differences?

### Nuance

Does the student avoid oversimplifying complicated topics?

### Structure

Is the answer understandable and logically organized?

### Independence

Does the answer appear to demonstrate understanding rather than just repeating a keyword?

Do NOT grade grammar harshly when grammar does not affect the subject knowledge.

For example:

A student may write:

"karma gör att det man gör påverkar vad som händer sen"

The AI should recognize that the student understands the basic concept even if the wording is informal.

---

# 12. DO NOT OVER-PENALIZE SHORT ANSWERS

The AI must distinguish between:

### Fact question

Short correct answer may be excellent.

### Reasoning question

A short answer may lack sufficient reasoning.

Therefore:

A student shouldn't receive a low result simply because they wrote few words on:

"What is Karma?"

But for:

"Resonera kring hur karma påverkar vardagslivet"

the AI should look for explanation, examples and connections.

# 13. AI FEEDBACK FORMAT

For every written question provide:

### Resultat

### Det du gjorde bra

"Du visar att du förstår sambandet mellan karma och handlingar."

### Det som saknas

"För ett mer utvecklat resonemang behöver du förklara hur detta kan påverka människors vardag och ge ett konkret exempel."

### Så kan du utveckla svaret

"Försök använda sambandet: handling → karma → framtida konsekvenser."

### Exempel på vad du kan träna på

**Karma + dharma + samsara**

Do NOT simply give the perfect answer immediately.

The purpose is learning.

# 14. GOLDEN FEEDBACK

# ⭐ Din gyllene feedback

**"Du har bra koll på centrala begrepp, särskilt karma, puja och högtider. Det som framför allt skulle lyfta dina svar är att utveckla dina resonemang. Du nämner ofta vad som händer, men nästa steg är att förklara varför det händer och vilka konsekvenser det får."**

Then:

### Nästa steg

1. Träna på att förklara "varför".
2. Använd fler ämnesbegrepp.
3. Ge konkreta exempel.
4. Jämför perspektiv istället för att bara beskriva dem.

Then:

**"Om du tränar på detta kommer dina resonemang sannolikt bli tydligare och mer utvecklade."**

Never guarantee that a student will receive a specific official grade.

# 15. SKOLVERKET-BASED ASSESSMENT ENGINE

Create an assessment framework based on the current **Skolverket Lgr22 criteria for the relevant subject and year level**.

Do NOT invent fake "official Examio grades."

For Årskurs 8, use the progression toward the end-of-year/Åk 9 criteria as an educational reference where appropriate.

For example, in Religionskunskap the current criteria describe progression such as:

E:

* grundläggande kunskaper
* enkla resonemang

C:

* goda kunskaper
* utvecklade resonemang

A:

* mycket goda kunskaper
* välutvecklade resonemang

D and B should be represented as intermediate overall levels.

Reference the actual current Skolverket criteria in the admin/configuration documentation rather than hardcoding unsupported claims.

The AI should assess dimensions separately:

* Faktakunskaper
* Begreppsanvändning
* Resonemang
* Jämförelser
* Orsak/konsekvens
* Ämnesspecifik förståelse

Then calculate an **Examio practice assessment**.

Do NOT simply calculate:

"80% = B"

because Swedish grading is not just percentage-based.

A student who gets many multiple-choice questions right but gives weak reasoning should not automatically receive an A-like assessment.

# FINAL PRODUCT VISION

Examio should feel like:

**Exam.net + intelligent study feedback + Swedish curriculum-aware practice + AI exam generation**

but with its own unique identity.

The biggest differentiator is NOT simply generating exams.

The biggest differentiator is:

**Examio understands HOW a student answers.**

It should be able to say:

"You know the facts, but your reasoning is often too short."

or:

"You use the correct concepts, but you don't yet connect them together."

or:

"You are good at explaining causes and consequences, but comparisons between perspectives need more development."

That is the experience I want.

Build it cleanly, professionally and realistically.

Do not create fake functionality that only looks like it works.

If an API, database or AI feature is not configured yet, create the correct architecture and a clear setup path rather than pretending it works.
